# Think Passage 2 break layer

The generator's **Break rhythm** preset controls note timing. The separate **Break Layer → Think Passage 2 · chopped** option adds audio from the bundled 1.42× passage to a dedicated **Think Break** tracker track without replacing the selected drum kit. It is off by default. Generate and Variation replace its generated notes; hand-edited and locked notes survive. The track is saved with the project and rendered by the same engine as Preview and WAV export.

The bundled mono WAV is decoded at its native 44,100 Hz. The slice map uses the original sample frames, without trimming or normalizing the source. The cut boundaries are `0, 8272, 16720, 25080, 29216, 33176, 37400, 41096, 49192, 57288, 69255`. The ten intervals are provisionally tagged as kick, hat, snare, hat, other, hat, other, hat, snare, hat, respectively. These are detector candidates refined by waveform and band-energy analysis, **not independently verified by listening**. Use the track's **Edit Think slices** control to audition and move a marker; edited maps persist and are reused on regeneration. Newly added slices inherit a provisional role from their position in the original map. Please listen to each slice before treating its role as curated. The generator uses any available slice when an edited map no longer contains a role-tagged note.

The separately mapped “uh” accent preserves the user-identified source passage from 0.690–1.445 seconds within Passage 2, corresponding to bundled frames 21,429–44,876. It overlaps the drum-slice map by design. The normal kit should generally be an ordinary one-shot kit; the old **Think Passage 2 · full clip (legacy)** kit option still retriggers the entire passage as a percussion hit and is identified as such for existing projects.

The layer takes its timing from the chosen genre engine's generated kick, snare, hat, and percussion notes. Anchor notes are prioritized; optional notes follow the seed, Complexity, Spicy, and Variation. Up to nine supporting slices are added per bar so busy styles remain bounded. The last bar receives the intact “uh” phrase as an answer to the backbeat. No model is trained by this feature, and the role labels remain provisional until a listening review.
