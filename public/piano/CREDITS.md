# Upright Piano KW

These 26 stereo piano recordings are a selected range of the FreePats Upright Piano KW bank, version 2022-02-21. The instrument was recorded by Gonzalo and Roberto from a Kawai upright piano. The original bank and its recording notes are at https://github.com/freepats/upright-piano-KW and https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW.

The creators published this bank under Creative Commons CC0 1.0. The accompanying `CC0-LICENSE.txt` is the repository's license text. The selection covers the register used by generated chords, with soft and strong recordings where supplied. Original FLAC audio is kept unchanged.
